Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on pacificeliterides.com, operated by Pacific Elite Rides LLC. For how we handle the information you give us when you book, see our Privacy Policy.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They help a site remember your choices and understand how it is used.

Cookies We Use

Essential. Your language choice (English / Español) and your cookie choice are saved in your browser so the site remembers them. These do not track you and cannot be turned off.

Analytics and advertising (Google). Google Analytics and Google Ads set cookies such as _ga to count visits, see which pages are useful, and measure whether our ads lead to booking requests or calls. Google may process this data as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Maps and forms. The booking form uses Google Maps for address suggestions and EmailJS to deliver your request to us. These services receive the information needed to work (for example, the address you type) and may set their own cookies under their own policies.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of our own.

Your Choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline analytics and advertising cookies. If you decline, or if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we turn them off. You can change your choice at any time:

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of personalized ads through Google's Ads Settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
