Terms of Service

Last updated: September 26, 2026

These terms govern your booking with Pacific Elite Rides, operated by Pacific Elite Rides LLC, a licensed and insured ground transportation provider in San Diego County, California. By submitting a booking request, you agree to the terms below.

Booking & Confirmation

A booking request submitted online or by phone is not confirmed until you receive confirmation from us with your driver, vehicle, and pickup time — typically within 15 minutes. We recommend booking at least 2 hours in advance for standard rides, and 24 hours in advance for groups, Sprinter van service, or special events.

Payment

We do not currently process payment online. Payment is due at the end of the ride by Zelle, Venmo, or card, or by an emailed invoice for corporate and event bookings, payable before or shortly after the ride. We do not accept cash. All quoted rates include fuel and tolls; gratuity is not included and is at your discretion.

Cancellations

Airport and point-to-point rides may be cancelled at no charge up to 2 hours before pickup. Sprinter van, group, and special-event bookings may be cancelled at no charge up to 48 hours before pickup. Cancellations after these windows, or no-shows, may be billed in full.

Refunds

If you prepaid (for example, by invoice) and cancel within the free cancellation window above, we refund the full amount to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. If we cancel your ride, or cannot provide the booked vehicle and you decline the substitute we offer, you receive a full refund. Late cancellations and no-shows are not refundable. Questions about a charge? Contact us within 7 days of your ride and we will reply within 2 business days.

Wait Time

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free wait time from actual flight landing. Point-to-point and hourly bookings include a grace period as agreed at booking. Additional wait time beyond that may be billed at the applicable hourly rate.

Flight Tracking

For airport transfers, we monitor your flight status and adjust your pickup time automatically for delays at no extra charge, provided accurate flight information was given at booking.

Passenger Conduct & Vehicle Care

Passengers are responsible for damage to the vehicle beyond normal wear caused by themselves or their guests during a ride, including cleaning fees for excessive mess. We reserve the right to end a ride early for unsafe or abusive conduct toward the driver.

Liability

Pacific Elite Rides carries commercial auto insurance and licensing as required by California law. We are not liable for delays caused by circumstances outside our control (traffic, weather, road closures), though we make every reasonable effort to arrive on time.

Text Messaging Program

Pacific Elite Rides booking texts. If you opt in on our booking form, or text us first, we will text you about your ride: quotes, booking confirmations, driver and vehicle details, pickup reminders and updates, and a follow-up after the ride. Message frequency varies by booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time; you will receive one confirmation text and no further messages.
